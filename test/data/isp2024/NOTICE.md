# Third-Party Data Notice

The files under `pisp-downloads/` in this directory are a small fixture
derived from the Australian Energy Market Operator's (AEMO) 2024
Integrated System Plan (ISP) material, used here strictly as a fixed,
version-controlled regression fixture for `ParseISP.jl`'s automated test
suite. This is **not** a complete copy of AEMO's ISP data, and the three
categories below are not uniformly "cropped" — see each one for what was
actually kept. Exact per-file provenance (`mode`, source/fixture SHA-256,
byte size) is recorded in `fixture-manifest.toml`.

## Source and categories

1. **Three root workbooks** (`2019-input-and-assumptions-workbook-v1-3-dec-19.xlsx`,
   `2023-iasr-ev-workbook.xlsx`, `2024-isp-inputs-and-assumptions-workbook.xlsx`,
   ~37.8MB) — complete, byte-for-byte, **unmodified** copies of AEMO
   publications (`mode = "copy"` in the manifest; `source_sha256 ==
   fixture_sha256`). These are original AEMO Material.
2. **883 trace/demand/hydro CSV files** (~2.1MB) — cropped extracts of
   AEMO-published solar, wind, demand, and hydro trace data, retaining
   only a handful of dates/years needed to exercise the six regression
   cases (`mode = "crop"` in the manifest). These are modified excerpts
   of AEMO Material.
3. **Six `Auxiliary/*.xlsx` workbooks** (`CapacityOutlook2024_Condensed.xlsx`,
   `StorageCapacityOutlook_2024_ISP.xlsx`, `StorageEnergyOutlook_2024_ISP.xlsx`,
   and the three `2024 ISP - <scenario> - Core_REZCAP.xlsx` files, ~4.0MB)
   — copied whole, byte-for-byte, from the maintainer's local collection,
   but these are **not AEMO publications**. They are preprocessing outputs
   generated by ParseISP.jl (or its predecessor PISP) itself from AEMO
   source data. The exact producer commit/command that generated them was
   not recovered when this fixture was built, so that provenance detail
   is recorded as a known gap rather than misattributed to AEMO.

Base AEMO publication: *2024 Integrated System Plan (ISP)* and associated
Inputs and Assumptions workbooks / trace data, copyright Australian
Energy Market Operator Limited (AEMO),
<https://www.aemo.com.au/energy-systems/major-publications/integrated-system-plan-isp/2024-integrated-system-plan-isp>.
